`pilot_lib.retry.retry(fn, attempts, backoff=0.0, sleep=None)` calls `fn` one time too few and sleeps a constant delay. It must call `fn` at most `attempts` times, return the first successful result, sleep between attempts only (never after the last), use exponential backoff (backoff, 2*backoff, 4*backoff, ...) when `sleep` is given, re-raise the last exception, and raise ValueError when attempts < 1.
