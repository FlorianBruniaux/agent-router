`pilot_lib.semver.semver_compare(a, b)` must follow Semantic Versioning 2.0.0 precedence: compare MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH numerically, a pre-release version (`1.0.0-alpha`) has lower precedence than the release, pre-release identifiers are compared dot by dot (numeric identifiers numerically, alphanumeric ones lexically, numeric lower than alphanumeric, a shorter set lower when all preceding identifiers are equal), and build metadata after `+` is ignored.
